About Me

I am a 2nd-year integrated MS/PhD student at KAIST AI, advised by Prof. Jong Chul Ye at BISPL Lab. Before KAIST, I earned a B.S. in Computer Science and a B.A. in Economics from Seoul National University.

My research focuses on reconstructing and generating the real 3D world. I am especially interested in streaming 3D reconstruction, geometric perception, Geometric Positional encoding, and camera-controlled video generation.
