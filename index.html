Streaming 3D Reconstruction

Latent State Modeling

Designing update rules and memory mechanisms that make recurrent visual geometry models more reliable beyond their training horizon.

Generative 3D Worlds

Exploring models that can synthesize, complete, and reason about realistic 3D structure from visual observations.
